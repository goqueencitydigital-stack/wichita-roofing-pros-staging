The Math

How the Estimate Is Calculated

No hidden formula. Three steps turn your footprint into a planning price.

  1. 1,800 sq ftHome footprint
  2. × 1.118Medium pitch factor
  3. 20.1 squaresRoof surface ÷ 100
  4. $10,163× $505 per square

Because a footprint does not capture overhangs, dormers or every roof plane, the calculator shows a ±10% range around footprint estimates. If you enter squares from an existing estimate or insurance scope, the result is a single number.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Still have a question? Call (316) 742-1900.

What is a roofing square?
One square equals 100 square feet of roof surface. A 25-square roof has about 2,500 square feet of roof surface.
How accurate is the calculator?
It is a planning estimate. Measurements and the final scope are confirmed at your free inspection, and your final price is provided in writing.
Why does my footprint get multiplied by a pitch factor?
A sloped roof has more surface than the ground it covers. A medium 6/12 roof has about 11.8% more surface than its footprint, and a 12/12 roof has about 41.4% more.
Should I include my garage in the footprint?
Yes. Include the attached garage and any covered porches that share the main roof, because they are roofed too.
How do I know my roof pitch?
Most Wichita homes are close to a medium 6/12 pitch. Ranch homes with gentle slopes are often lower, and two-story homes with tall gables are often steeper. We measure the exact pitch during the inspection.
Does the estimate include tear-off?
Ask during your inspection; the written estimate lists the full scope.
Can I finance my roof?
Wichita Roofing Pros offers financing through Wisetack for qualifying roofing projects, from $500 to $25,000*, with terms of 3 to 60 months* and 0–35.9% APR* based on creditworthiness. Checking options uses a soft credit check. *All financing is subject to credit approval.
Does a new roof come with a warranty?
Yes. New roofs include our lifetime transferable workmanship warranty.
