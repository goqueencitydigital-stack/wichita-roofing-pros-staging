What Is LP SmartSide Siding?

LP SmartSide is an engineered wood siding product made using advanced technology to improve strength and resistance. Unlike traditional wood siding, it is treated to withstand moisture, pests, and extreme weather conditions.

This siding option combines the natural look of wood with modern durability, making it ideal for homeowners who want both beauty and performance.

Common Problems with Traditional Siding

Before understanding the benefits, it’s important to recognize the issues many homeowners face with outdated siding materials.

Moisture Damage

Traditional wood siding absorbs moisture, leading to rot, swelling, and mold growth.

Pest Infestation

Termites and other insects can easily damage untreated wood siding.

Cracking and Warping

Temperature changes can cause siding to expand and contract, resulting in visible damage.

High Maintenance

Frequent painting, sealing, and repairs are required to maintain appearance and function.

Poor Energy Efficiency

Damaged or aging siding can allow air leaks, increasing heating and cooling costs.

Designed to withstand harsh weather while enhancing curb appeal, this engineered wood siding has become a top choice for homeowners looking for durability and style.

How LP SmartSide Solves These Issues

Upgrading to engineered wood siding like LP SmartSide addresses these common problems effectively.

Enhanced Moisture Resistance

The material is treated with special resins that help prevent water absorption and decay.

Built-In Pest Protection

It resists termites and fungal decay, ensuring long-term durability.

Impact Resistance

Designed to withstand hail, debris, and everyday wear, making it ideal for unpredictable weather.

Low Maintenance

Requires less upkeep compared to traditional wood, saving time and money.

Improved Insulation

Helps maintain indoor temperatures, contributing to better energy efficiency.

Signs It’s Time to Replace Your Siding

Not sure if you need an upgrade? Look for these warning signs:

  • Peeling or fading paint
  • Visible cracks or gaps
  • Increased energy bills
  • Mold or mildew growth
  • Frequent repairs

If you notice any of these issues, it may be time to consider a siding replacement.

Even the best siding material won’t perform well if it’s not installed correctly.

Proper Measurements

Ensures a perfect fit and prevents gaps.

Secure Fastening

Weatherproof Sealing

Protects against moisture and air leaks.

Compliance with Building Codes

Hiring experienced professionals guarantees that your siding investment delivers maximum value.

Maintenance Tips for Long-Lasting Performance

Although LP SmartSide requires less maintenance, a few simple steps can extend its lifespan:

  • Inspect for damage after severe weather
  • Repaint or touch up when needed
  • Keep gutters clean to prevent water overflow

Regular care helps maintain both appearance and functionality.

How siding and roofing work together

Siding, trim, flashing and the roof edge form one water-shedding system. When we see wall stains near a roof line, we check kick-out flashing, step flashing and siding together before recommending a fix.

Siding warning signs

Look closely at the sides that face prevailing wind and storms.

  • Cracked, split or hail-dented panels
  • Warped or buckled sections
  • Soft or swollen boards near the ground or roof line
  • Peeling paint on trim
  • Gaps where siding meets windows or roof lines
  • Mold or mildew streaks
  • Loose or missing pieces after wind
  • Interior wall stains on exterior walls

How we evaluate siding damage

We look beyond the panel to the water path behind it.

  1. Walk every elevation. Note cracks, dents and gaps.
  2. Check transitions. Windows, doors, roof lines and kick-out flashing.
  3. Probe soft spots. Look for swelling or rot.
  4. Review trim and soffit. Ventilation and edge condition.
  5. Recommend. Patch, section replace or full re-side.

What affects siding cost

Wall area, material, number of stories, trim and soffit work, and any sheathing repair. Matching existing siding can limit patch options. You receive a written scope and price after the inspection.

FAQs

1. How long does LP SmartSide siding last?
It can last over 20–30 years with proper installation and maintenance.

2. Is LP SmartSide better than vinyl siding?
Yes, it offers greater durability, impact resistance, and a more natural wood appearance.

3. Does it require frequent maintenance?
No, it requires minimal upkeep compared to traditional wood siding.

4. Can LP SmartSide withstand harsh weather?
Yes, it is designed to resist moisture, wind, and impact damage.

5. Is professional installation necessary?
Yes, proper installation ensures maximum performance and longevity.

Does hail damage siding?

Yes. Hail can crack vinyl and dent or chip other materials. Document it along with roof damage.

Do you replace soffit and fascia?

Yes. Wichita Roofing Pros repairs and replaces soffit, fascia and trim.

Should siding be done before or after a roof?

Usually the roof first, then siding and gutters, so flashing and edges tie in correctly.
