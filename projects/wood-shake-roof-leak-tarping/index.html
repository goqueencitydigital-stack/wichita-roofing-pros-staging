Wood Shake Roof Leak Tarping During an Insurance Claim

Dan tarps active leak spots on a wood shake roof while the homeowner’s insurance claim is underway, keeping interior damage from getting worse until the roof can be replaced.

Schedule a Free Roof InspectionCall (316) 742-1900
Project Details
Scope
Emergency leak tarping
Material
Existing wood shake roof; synthetic underlayment tarp with cap nails
Location
Not stated

What materials are used for tarping?

A roofing hammer, plastic-cap roofing nails and synthetic underlayment. The cap keeps water from running down the nail head.

Why tarp during a claim?

Tarping documents and limits further damage while the claim is open. If tarps start to degrade, the crew comes back and re-tarps.
