The original gray shingles on this brick ranch were weathered along the ridge and edges. The crew tore the roof off, installed new architectural shingles and a straight new ridge line, and the home was photographed again from the same spots after completion.

What did the roof look like before?

The original gray shingle roof showed weathering at the ridge, roof edges and across the slopes. Photos taken on May 20, 2026 document the condition from the driveway, front lawn and on the roof.

02

What work was done?

The crew completed a full tear-off and installed new architectural asphalt shingles, ridge cap and edge details. Process photos show the shingle courses going on slope by slope.

What changed?

The after photos, taken from matching angles, show a uniform tan architectural shingle roof with a straight ridge line and clean edges.

When do I need roof replacement?

Replacement becomes more likely when material deterioration is widespread, leaks continue in multiple areas, decking is damaged, weather exposure has affected larger portions of the roof, or repeated repairs are no longer providing dependable protection. Full details.

How much does replacing a roof cost?

The cost to replace a roof on a house project depends on roof size, pitch, material selection, removal requirements, decking condition, flashing, ventilation, accessibility, and the final approved scope. An inspection is needed before a meaningful property-specific estimate can be prepared. Full details.

When is the best time to replace a roof?

The best time to replace roof systems is when safe installation conditions are available and before known deterioration causes additional decking or interior damage. Urgency, weather forecasts, material availability, and scheduling can all influence timing. Full details.

What time of year is best for replacement?

The best time of year for roof replacement is not identical for every Wichita property. The condition of the existing system, current weather, project urgency, and the risk of delaying necessary work are more useful factors than choosing a season by itself. Full details.

Does a new roof come with a warranty?

New roofs include a lifetime transferable workmanship warranty, with TAMKO system upgrades available. See the warranty details.
