Why load the roof first?

Materials are loaded on the roof before the crew arrives so roofers are not carrying bundles up ladders all day. In the video, Dan estimates it speeds up the job by about three hours.

What is checked after tear-off?

Once shingles and tar paper are off, the decking is inspected to confirm a solid, nailable surface with no rot before the new roof goes on.

How is the property protected?

Tarps catch debris on the driveway, and the crew runs magnets multiple times to pick up nails.
