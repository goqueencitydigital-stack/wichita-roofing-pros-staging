FAQ

Got any questions? We’ve got answers.

Still have a question? Call (316) 742-1900.

How do I know if I need a roof replacement?

If your roof has frequent leaks, missing shingles, or visible sagging, it may be time for a replacement. A professional inspection can confirm the exact condition and help you decide.

How long does a new roof installation take?

Most residential roof installations take 1–3 days depending on size and weather conditions. Larger or more complex roofs may take slightly longer.

Do you offer financing for roofing projects?

Yes, financing options are available to help make roof repairs and replacements more affordable. You can choose flexible monthly payment plans based on your budget.

What types of roofing materials do you install?

We install a variety of materials including asphalt shingles, metal roofing, and synthetic slate. Each option offers different benefits for durability, cost, and appearance.

Do you provide free roof inspections?

Yes, we offer free roof inspections to identify damage, leaks, or maintenance needs. This helps homeowners make informed decisions without any upfront cost.
