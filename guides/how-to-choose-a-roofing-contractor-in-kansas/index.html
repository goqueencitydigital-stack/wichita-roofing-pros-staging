How Do You Choose a Roofing Contractor in Kansas?

Published 2026-11-20By the Wichita Roofing Pros teamHomeowner Guide
In this guideWhat should you check before hiring?How do you spot a storm chaser?Where can you check Wichita Roofing Pros?Frequently asked questions

Quick answerChoose a contractor registered with the Kansas Attorney General, carrying liability insurance, with a local address you can visit and a written scope and warranty. Check reviews and references, and be cautious of anyone who pressures you to sign quickly after a storm or asks for full payment upfront.

What should you check before hiring?

These checks take minutes and filter out most problems.

  1. Kansas AG registration. Look the company up in the Attorney General’s roofing registration directory.
  2. Insurance. Kansas registration requires proof of liability insurance of not less than $500,000.
  3. Local presence. A real office and phone number — ours is 1809 S Hoover Rd Suite 101, Wichita.
  4. Reviews and references. Read recent reviews and ask for nearby jobs.
  5. Written scope and warranty. Materials, tear-off, decking, flashing, cleanup and workmanship coverage in writing.

How do you spot a storm chaser?

After big storms, out-of-area crews knock on doors across Wichita. The FTC warns about door-to-door offers, high-pressure tactics and large upfront payments.

  • Out-of-state plates and no local office
  • Offers to waive or cover your deductible
  • Asks you to sign before an inspection or adjuster visit
  • Wants full payment upfront

Frequently asked questions

Is a roofing license required in Kansas?

Kansas requires roofing contractor registration through the Attorney General rather than a separate state license. Local jurisdictions may also have requirements.

Should I pay a deposit?

A reasonable deposit can be normal; full payment before work starts is a warning sign according to the FTC.
