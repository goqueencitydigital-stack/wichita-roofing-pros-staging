How Long Does a Roof Replacement Take?

Published 2026-11-20By the Wichita Roofing Pros teamHomeowner Guide
In this guideWhat happens on installation day?What can slow a replacement down?How should you prepare?Frequently asked questions

Quick answerMost residential roof replacements can often be completed within one to a few days, depending on the size of the roof, weather and the condition of the decking. Scheduling, materials and permits come first, so the full timeline from inspection to final walkthrough is longer than the installation itself.

What happens on installation day?

Installation follows the same order on every roof. In our walkthrough video, the crew had the materials loaded on the roof before tear-off to speed up the day.

  1. Protect the property. Tarps go down on the driveway and landscaping.
  2. Tear off. Old shingles and underlayment come off to the deck.
  3. Inspect the deck. We confirm a solid, nailable surface or replace damaged decking.
  4. Install. Underlayment, flashing, shingles, vents and ridge cap.
  5. Clean up. Magnet sweeps for nails and debris haul-off.
  6. Walkthrough. We review the finished roof with you.

What can slow a replacement down?

Weather is the most common delay; crews do not tear off roofs into incoming rain or high wind. Hidden decking damage, very steep or large roofs and material availability can also add time.

How should you prepare?

Move vehicles out of the driveway, take fragile items off walls (hammering vibrates them), and keep pets and children away from the work area.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to be home during the roof replacement?

Not necessarily. We confirm access, parking and pets beforehand, and review the finished work with you at the walkthrough.

Can you replace a roof in winter?

Yes, on suitable days. Shingle sealing and crew safety depend on temperature and conditions, so scheduling stays flexible.

The Full Timeline

Installation is usually the shortest part. Here is how the whole process usually unfolds.

Free inspection

We inspect and photograph the roof, usually in about 30 to 60 minutes, and explain what we found. Scheduling the inspection itself is usually quick.

02

Written estimate

You receive a written scope with measured roof size, materials, tear-off, decking pricing, ventilation, flashing, cleanup and warranty.

03

Insurance (if a claim)

Storm claims add the adjuster visit and claim decision. That timing depends on your insurer and how busy they are after a storm, not on us.

04

Material selection and order

You choose shingle line and color. We confirm product availability and schedule delivery.

05

Permit

Where a permit is required, it is pulled before work begins. Wichita and Sedgwick County permits go through MABCD.

06

Install, cleanup and walkthrough

Most homes take one to a few days on site, followed by cleanup, a final walkthrough and your warranty paperwork.

Roof Size and Complexity

How do roof size and shape change installation time?

Two homes with the same square footage can take very different amounts of time.

The biggest factor is the number of roofing squares (one square is 100 square feet of roof surface). A simple ranch roof with two large planes goes faster than a two-story roof with dormers, valleys and several small sections, even if the total area is similar. Steeper roofs require more safety setup and slower movement, so they take longer.

Other factors include:

  • How many layers of old roofing must come off
  • How much decking needs to be replaced once the old roof is removed
  • Chimneys, skylights and walls that need new flashing
  • Access for the dumpster and material delivery
  • Whether gutters, fascia or ventilation work is part of the job

You can estimate your roof size in squares with the roof cost calculator or learn more in What is a roofing square?

Weather

What happens if the weather changes during my roof replacement?

We watch the forecast closely and only open as much of the roof as the crew can dry in that day. If rain or high wind moves in, the exposed areas are covered and the work resumes when conditions are safe. Wind matters for safety and for shingles sealing properly. Very cold weather can slow sealing, which is why installation methods may change in winter.

Storm season can also affect the schedule. After a major hail event, many Wichita homeowners call at once. If your roof is leaking, we can help with temporary protection like emergency tarping while the full replacement is scheduled.

Install Day

What should you expect while the crew is at your home?

A little preparation makes the day easier for everyone.

01

Noise and vibration

Tear-off and nailing are loud. Remove wall hangings on upper floors and keep pets somewhere calm.

02

Driveway and access

Move vehicles out of the garage and driveway the night before so the crew can reach the dumpster and materials.

03

Attic items

Small debris can fall through gaps in old decking. Cover items stored in the attic.

04

Yard and landscaping

Tarps protect landscaping, and a magnetic sweep picks up nails at the end of the day. Mark sprinkler heads or delicate plants for the crew.

Hour by Hour

What does a typical one-day roof replacement look like?

Every roof is different, but a straightforward one-day replacement usually follows this rhythm.

Early morning: The crew arrives, introduces the lead, and sets tarps over landscaping, the driveway and anything close to the house. The dumpster or trailer is positioned where debris can drop straight into it.

Morning: Tear-off starts on the first section. Old shingles, underlayment and worn flashing come off down to the deck. As soon as the deck is exposed, it is checked for soft or rotten boards. Any damaged decking is shown to you and replaced at the price listed in your estimate.

Midday: The bare deck is covered with underlayment and ice and water protection where needed, so the roof is protected even before shingles go on. Drip edge goes on at the eaves and rakes.

Afternoon: Starter strips, field shingles, flashing, vents and ridge caps are installed section by section. The crew follows the manufacturer’s nailing pattern so the shingles seal properly and the product warranty stays valid.

End of day: Debris is hauled away, gutters are cleared of loose material, and a magnetic sweeper runs across the yard and driveway to pick up nails. We walk the finished roof with you, answer questions and make sure you know what paperwork is coming.

Larger or more complex roofs follow the same order over two or more days. Any part of the roof that is open at the end of a day is dried in before the crew leaves.

Planning Ahead

When is the best time of year to schedule a roof replacement in Wichita?

There is no single best month. Spring and early summer bring the most storm damage, so schedules fill quickly after big hail events. Late summer and fall usually offer steady weather and good conditions for shingles to seal. Winter work is possible on many days with the right methods, and it can offer more scheduling flexibility.

If your roof is aging but not leaking, the best time is usually before it becomes urgent. Planning ahead lets you choose materials and colors without pressure, compare estimates and line up financing if you need it. If your roof is leaking now, call (316) 742-1900 and we will help you protect the home first.

Insurance Timelines

How long does an insurance roof replacement take from claim to install?

Insurance roof replacements have more steps before the crew arrives. A typical order looks like this:

  • Storm and inspection: you notice damage or schedule a free inspection after a storm.
  • Claim filed: you contact your insurer and receive a claim number.
  • Adjuster visit: the insurer inspects the roof. You can ask us to be there to point out damage.
  • Claim decision: the insurer sends its estimate and any first payment.
  • Materials and scheduling: you choose shingles and color, and we schedule installation.
  • Installation and closeout: the roof is installed, and any supplements or final paperwork are sent to the insurer.

The steps your insurer controls usually take the longest, especially after a widespread hail event when adjusters are busy. Keeping your claim number, adjuster’s name and every insurer document together helps things move faster. Read our insurance claim roof replacement page for more.

More timeline questions

Do I have to be home during my roof replacement?

No. Many homeowners go about their day. We do ask for a way to reach you, and we recommend being available for the final walkthrough.

Can a roof replacement be done in one day?

Many smaller or simpler roofs can be finished in a single day. Larger, steeper or more complex roofs, or roofs that need significant decking work, take longer.

How long does an insurance roof replacement take?

The installation takes about as long as any replacement. The overall timeline is longer because the adjuster visit, claim approval and any supplements come first.

Will my home be exposed overnight?

We plan the day so open areas are dried in before the crew leaves. If a job spans more than one day, any unfinished sections are protected.

What happens if the crew finds rotten decking?

We show you the damage, replace the affected decking at the price written in your estimate, and continue. Decking pricing is listed up front so there are no surprises.

Is it safe to stay home during a roof replacement?

Yes. It is loud, so most people with small children, pets or work-from-home calls plan to be out for part of the day, but staying home is safe.

Does Wichita Roofing Pros work on weekends?

Our office is open Monday–Friday 8AM–7PM and Saturday 10AM–6PM. Installation days are scheduled with you in advance.

How far in advance should I schedule a roof replacement?

It depends on the season and demand. After major storms, schedules fill quickly. Call early so you can choose materials and lock in a date that works for you.

Can my roof be replaced the same week I call?

Sometimes, depending on the season, material availability and permits. After major storms, schedules are longer. Call (316) 742-1900 for current timing.

Does replacing gutters add time to a roof job?

A little. Gutter work is often done right after the roof, sometimes on the same day, and is scheduled as part of the project.
