Asphalt, Metal or Synthetic Slate: Which Roof Fits a Wichita Home?

Published 2026-11-20By the Wichita Roofing Pros teamHomeowner Guide
In this guideHow do the three materials compare?When are asphalt shingles the right choice?When is metal roofing worth it?Who should consider synthetic slate or shake?How does impact resistance fit in?Frequently asked questions

Quick answerAsphalt shingles are the most common choice for Wichita homes because they balance cost, appearance and repairability. Metal roofing suits homeowners planning to stay long-term who want a different look and fewer roof replacements. Synthetic slate and shake give a high-end look at a lighter weight than natural stone or wood.

How do the three materials compare?

Each material is a trade-off between upfront cost, appearance and long-term upkeep. The table summarizes how they usually compare on a Wichita home.

Residential roofing materials comparison
Asphalt shinglesMetal roofingSynthetic slate / shake
LookArchitectural, dimensional shingleStanding seam or panel linesSlate or cedar shake appearance
Upfront costLowest of the threeHigherHighest of the three
Hail optionsImpact-resistant Class 3 and Class 4 lines availableCan dent cosmetically in large hailMany products carry impact ratings
RepairsSimple, widely availablePanel-based, specializedProduct-specific
Best forMost homes and budgetsLong-term owners wanting a modern lookHistoric or high-end homes

Commercial low-slope systems (TPO, modified bitumen) are compared separately on the roofing materials page.

When are asphalt shingles the right choice?

Asphalt is the right fit when you want a proven roof at the most practical price, with easy repairs after storms. Architectural shingles add depth and wind performance over older 3-tab styles.

Our walkthrough project used TAMKO Titan XT Class 3 impact-resistant shingles in Weathered Wood. See the full tear-off walkthrough.

When is metal roofing worth it?

Metal is worth considering if you plan to stay in the home for decades and like its clean look. Large hail can dent metal cosmetically, so ask how the panel warranty treats cosmetic damage.

Who should consider synthetic slate or shake?

Synthetic slate and shake suit homeowners who want the look of natural slate or cedar without the weight and upkeep. They are a common choice on historic-style homes and in neighborhoods with strict appearance standards.

How does impact resistance fit in?

Impact-resistant shingles are tested against hail-sized steel balls; Class 4 is the highest rating. They reduce damage from small and medium hail, but very large hail can still damage any roof.

Read Class 3 vs. Class 4 shingles. Research from the Insurance Institute for Business & Home Safety shows impact performance varies between products.

Frequently asked questions

Is metal roofing louder in the rain?

Over a solid deck with underlayment and an insulated attic, most homeowners find metal roofs no louder than shingles. Open-framed outbuildings are a different story.

Which roofing material holds up best to Wichita hail?

Impact-rated products hold up best to small and medium hail. No residential material is hail-proof against very large stones.

Can I switch from shingles to metal?

Usually, yes. The inspection confirms the decking and framing, and the scope covers flashing and trim changes needed for metal.
