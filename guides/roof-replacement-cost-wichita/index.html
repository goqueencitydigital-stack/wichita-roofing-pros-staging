How Much Does Roof Replacement Cost in Wichita, KS?

Published 2026-11-20By the Wichita Roofing Pros teamHomeowner Guide

Quick answerRoof replacement with Wichita Roofing Pros starts from a basic planning price of $505 per roofing square, where one square equals 100 square feet of roof surface. A 25-square roof works out to about $12,625 at that rate. Your final price is confirmed in writing after a free inspection, because roof size, layers, decking, flashing and material choice change the total.

How is a roof replacement priced?

Roofs are priced by the square, which is 100 square feet of roof surface — not the floor area of the house. Roof surface is larger than your home’s footprint because of pitch and overhangs.

How do you want to measure?

This is a planning estimate at a basic price of $505 per square. It is not a bid. Roof size, layers to remove, decking condition, flashing, ventilation and material choice are confirmed during the free inspection, and your final price is provided in writing.

What changes the final price?

The per-square price is the starting point; the inspection confirms what your roof actually needs. These are the factors most likely to move the number.

  • Measured roof size and waste for hips, valleys and cut-up roof planes
  • Pitch and access — steeper roofs take more time and safety equipment
  • Number of shingle layers to tear off
  • Decking replacement if rot or soft spots are found
  • Flashing, pipe boots, ventilation and drip edge
  • Material choice: architectural shingles, impact-resistant shingles, metal or synthetic slate
  • Gutters, fascia or siding work added to the project

How do pitch and footprint affect roof size?

A steeper roof has more surface over the same footprint. A medium 6/12 pitch adds about 12% to the footprint; a 12/12 pitch adds about 41%.

Does insurance change what I pay?

If storm damage is covered under your policy, your insurer decides what it pays and you are typically responsible for your deductible. We document damage and can meet the adjuster, but coverage decisions belong to the insurance company.

See the Storm & Hail Resource Center for who does what.

Can I finance a roof replacement?

Yes. Financing is available through Wisetack for qualifying projects from $500–$25,000*, with terms from 3–60 months* and offers ranging from 0–35.9% APR* based on creditworthiness. All financing is subject to credit approval.

See financing details · Prequalify with Wisetack

Do I need a permit, and does that cost extra?

Roofing permits in Wichita and Sedgwick County are issued by MABCD. The published roofing permit fee is $0.05 per square foot, with a $50 minimum and $1,500 maximum.

Read the permit guide.

Frequently asked questions

What is a roofing square?

One roofing square is 100 square feet of roof surface. A 2,500 square-foot roof surface is 25 squares.

Is $505 per square the final price?

No. It is a basic planning price. Your final price is provided in writing after a free inspection confirms roof size, layers, decking and the material you choose.

Why do two roofing quotes differ so much?

Quotes differ in measured size, tear-off, decking allowances, flashing and ventilation work, materials and warranty. Compare the written scope line by line, not just the total.
