Does a Transferable Roof Warranty Help When You Sell Your Home?

Published 2026-11-20By the Wichita Roofing Pros teamHomeowner Guide
In this guideWhat is the difference between workmanship and manufacturer coverage?How do you transfer the warranty when you sell?Which TAMKO tiers are available with a new roof?What do buyers and agents usually ask about the roof?Frequently asked questions

Quick answerYes. A transferable roof warranty gives buyers written proof of who stands behind the roof, which can answer roof questions during inspections and disclosures. Wichita Roofing Pros’ lifetime transferable workmanship warranty can move to the next owner once, as long as the transfer is requested within 30 days of the sale and a warranty inspection is completed.

What is the difference between workmanship and manufacturer coverage?

The manufacturer warranty covers defects in the roofing products. The workmanship warranty covers covered problems caused by how the roof was installed, and that is the contractor’s responsibility.

“Simply put, the manufacturer stands behind its product. Wichita Roofing Pros stands behind our installation.”

From our warranty video

How do you transfer the warranty when you sell?

The transfer follows a short written process. Miss the window and the coverage may not move to the buyer.

  1. Notify us within 30 days of the property sale.
  2. Schedule the warranty inspection. We confirm the roof’s condition for the new owner.
  3. Complete the transfer. The workmanship warranty can be transferred one time.

What do buyers and agents usually ask about the roof?

Kansas seller disclosure forms ask about roof age, leaks and repairs. Having the installation date, invoice, warranty certificate and transfer instructions ready makes those answers simple.

See the roof questions on the REALTORS of South Central Kansas seller’s disclosure, and read whether replacement is worth it before selling.

Frequently asked questions

How many times can the workmanship warranty be transferred?

One time, to the next owner, when the transfer is requested within 30 days of the sale and the warranty inspection is completed.

Does the warranty cover hail damage?

No. Workmanship warranties cover installation problems, not storm damage. Hail and wind damage are typically handled through homeowners insurance.

Do I have to maintain the roof to keep the warranty?

Yes. The warranty maintenance terms call for an annual inspection. Ask about our roof maintenance programs.
