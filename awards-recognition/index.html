Is Wichita Roofing Pros accredited?

Quick answerYes. Wichita Roofing Pros is a BBB Accredited Business with an A+ rating, BBB Torch Award winner, TAMKO Pro Platinum contractor, CertainTeed ShingleMaster, and member of WABA and NAHB.

Is Wichita Roofing Pros BBB accredited?

Yes. Wichita Roofing Pros is a BBB Accredited Business with an A+ rating and is a BBB Torch Award winner.
