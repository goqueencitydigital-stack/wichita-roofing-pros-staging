Overview

Wichita Roofing Pros provides roof replacement services for homeowners who need a clear answer about whether an aging or damaged roof can still be repaired or is ready for a complete change. We evaluate roof age, decking condition, flashing, valleys, ventilation, gutters, and the pattern of previous repairs before recommending work. Wichita hail, straight-line wind, heavy rain, and summer heat can accelerate roofing wear, so we base recommendations on visible conditions, documented findings, and what makes sense for the home long term.

Quick answerRoof replacement removes the old roof down to the deck and installs a new roof system: underlayment, flashing, ventilation and shingles or other roofing. Our basic planning price is $505 per roofing square, with your final price confirmed in writing after a free inspection. Most residential replacements can often be completed within one to a few days.

Symptoms

What signs mean you should call?

These are the signs homeowners most often notice first. Any one of them is worth a free inspection.

  • Roof near the end of its expected life
  • Hail or wind damage across several slopes
  • Widespread curling, cracking or granule loss
  • Repeated leaks in different places
  • Soft decking or sagging areas
Evaluation

How do we evaluate the problem?

Every inspection follows the same order so the recommendation is based on the whole roof, not the first symptom.

  1. Start with your concern. We inspect the spot that made you call.
  2. Inspect outward. Surrounding materials, flashing, valleys, vents, drainage and edges.
  3. Document. Photos when access is safe.
  4. Explain options. What needs attention now, what can be monitored, and why.
  5. Write it down. Scope and price in writing before work starts.

Replacement Day

What happens on roof replacement day?

Here is how a typical replacement runs from start to finish.

01

Protect the property

We cover landscaping, place tarps and position a dumpster or trailer where it will not damage your driveway or lawn.

02

Tear off the old roof

Old shingles, underlayment and worn flashing come off down to the decking.

03

Inspect and repair decking

We check the decking and replace soft or damaged boards so the new roof has a solid base.

04

Install underlayment and flashing

New underlayment, ice and water protection, drip edge and flashing go on before shingles.

05

Install the new roof

Shingles are installed following the manufacturer’s instructions, with ridge caps and ventilation.

06

Clean up and walk through

We clean up, run magnets for nails and walk the property with you.

Planning Ahead

How should you prepare for a roof replacement?

A few simple steps make replacement day easier for everyone.

Move vehicles. Park cars away from the driveway and garage area so crews can access the roof and dumpster safely.

Protect items in the attic. Vibration from tear-off can shake dust and debris loose. Cover or move stored items directly under the roof.

Take down wall hangings. Pictures and mirrors on top-floor walls can shift from hammering vibration.

Plan for pets and kids. The work is noisy. Keep pets inside or away from the work area, and keep children clear of the yard while crews are working.

Mark sprinklers and features. Let us know about sprinkler heads, septic covers, satellite dishes and solar equipment so we can plan around them.

What Should Wichita Homeowners Know About Roof Replacement?

A residential roof replacement should begin with a condition-based inspection, not an assumption that every leak requires a new roof. We look for widespread material wear, recurring leaks, soft or deteriorated decking, ventilation concerns, damaged flashing, and problems affecting multiple roof areas.

A ceiling stain may begin at a pipe boot, valley, fastener, wall transition, or flashing gap several feet from where water becomes visible indoors. That is why we inspect the surrounding system before deciding whether another targeted repair remains responsible or replacing the roof is the more dependable long-term choice.

Homeowners often search for signs you need a new roof when problems become more frequent. Repeated leaks, widespread material deterioration, damaged decking, multiple repair areas, and recurring moisture problems are stronger indicators than one isolated defect.

  • We evaluate roof age, material condition, decking, flashing, valleys, ventilation, gutters, and previous repair areas.
  • We distinguish isolated damage from deterioration affecting larger sections of the system.
  • We document visible concerns when roof access and conditions allow.
  • We explain what needs attention now and what can reasonably be monitored.

What other roofing work might need attention?

A replacement project may connect to specialty services without making every one of those services necessary. Depending on the condition and approved scope, homeowners may also need full tear-off work, insurance-related replacement assistance, or financing. Those services should be evaluated separately so the main roofing decision remains focused on the property itself.

Choosing among roof replacement options also requires looking at existing materials, roof slope, decking, ventilation, drainage, flashing, and how much of the surrounding system remains serviceable.

  • Decking that has deteriorated may require selective replacement before the new roof covering is installed.
  • Flashing, fascia, gutters, siding transitions, or ventilation may need attention when they contribute to water movement.
  • A re-roofing contractor should explain what is included in the approved work and what conditions could affect the final scope.
  • If a focused repair remains the responsible solution, the recommendation should stay focused on that repair.

Premium Products

What details matter most for roof replacement?

01

Material Condition

Age, impact damage, installation quality, roof pitch, and the extent of deterioration all influence replacement planning. New roof prices can differ even between similarly sized homes because decking, flashing, ventilation, removal requirements, material selection, and accessibility may vary significantly.
02

Storm Exposure

Wichita hail, straight-line winds, and heavy rain can affect shingles, vents, gutters, flashing, and other components during the same weather event. Storm history provides useful context, but recommendations should be based on conditions that can actually be observed and evaluated.
03

Flashing, Valleys, and Ventilation

Transitions around walls, valleys, chimneys, vents, penetrations, edges, and attic airflow affect long-term performance. These details should be evaluated before the approved scope is finalized.
04

Connected Exterior Work

If water is moving behind gutters, deteriorating fascia, or reaching siding, replacing the roof covering alone may not correct the entire moisture path. Connected exterior components should be reviewed when they directly affect roof performance.

Common Questions

Roof Replacement FAQ

Still have a question? Call (316) 742-1900.

When do I need roof replacement?
Replacement becomes more likely when material deterioration is widespread, leaks continue in multiple areas, decking is damaged, weather exposure has affected larger portions of the roof, or repeated repairs are no longer providing dependable protection.
How much does replacing a roof cost?
The cost to replace a roof on a house project depends on roof size, pitch, material selection, removal requirements, decking condition, flashing, ventilation, accessibility, and the final approved scope. An inspection is needed before a meaningful property-specific estimate can be prepared.
When is the best time to replace a roof?
The best time to replace roof systems is when safe installation conditions are available and before known deterioration causes additional decking or interior damage. Urgency, weather forecasts, material availability, and scheduling can all influence timing.
What time of year is best for replacement?
The best time of year for roof replacement is not identical for every Wichita property. The condition of the existing system, current weather, project urgency, and the risk of delaying necessary work are more useful factors than choosing a season by itself.
What does a new roof include?
The exact scope depends on the property and proposal, but a complete project may involve removal of existing roofing, underlayment, new roofing materials, flashing, ventilation components, necessary decking work, disposal, cleanup, and final documentation. Each included item should be clearly identified before work begins.

Can I finance my roof replacement?

How long does a roof replacement take?
It depends on the size of the roof, the weather and the number of layers to remove. We give you the expected schedule before work begins.
Do I need to be home during the replacement?
You do not need to be home the whole time, but we ask to review the plan with you before work begins and walk the property afterward.
What warranty comes with a new roof?
New roofs include our lifetime transferable workmanship warranty, plus the manufacturer’s product warranty.
Can a roof be replaced in winter?
Roofs can be replaced in cooler weather with the right precautions. Some shingles seal best in warmer temperatures, and we plan the work around the forecast.
What happens if it rains during my replacement?
We watch the forecast closely and only open as much roof as we can cover the same day. If rain moves in, crews secure the roof before leaving.
