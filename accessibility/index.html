Accessibility Statement

Accessibility Commitment

At Wichita Roofing Pros, we are committed to ensuring digital accessibility for all users, including individuals with disabilities. We continually work to improve the user experience for everyone and apply relevant accessibility standards to enhance usability across our website.

Our Accessibility Efforts

We strive to make our website accessible and user-friendly by:

  • Using clear and readable fonts and content structure
  • Providing keyboard-friendly navigation
  • Maintaining color contrast for better readability
  • Adding descriptive alternative text for images where appropriate
  • Ensuring our website functions across different devices and screen sizes
  • Regularly reviewing website accessibility and usability improvements

Ongoing Improvements

Accessibility is an ongoing process, and we are continuously working to improve the accessibility and usability of our website. We aim to follow recognized best practices and standards, including the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG).

Third-Party Content

While we strive to ensure accessibility throughout our website, some third-party integrations or external links may not fully meet accessibility standards. We encourage users to contact us if they encounter any accessibility barriers.

Need Assistance?

If you experience difficulty accessing any part of this website or need assistance with any of our services, please contact us. We value your feedback and will make reasonable efforts to accommodate your needs.
