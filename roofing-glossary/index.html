Roofing Glossary

Plain-English definitions of the words you will see on an estimate or inspection report.

3-tab shingles
Flat, single-layer asphalt shingles with three cutouts per strip; an older, lighter style.
Adjuster
The insurance company’s representative who inspects damage and recommends what the policy covers.
Architectural shingles
Laminated, dimensional asphalt shingles with a thicker profile and better wind performance than 3-tab shingles.
Class 4 shingle
A shingle that passes the UL 2218 impact test with a 2-inch steel ball, the highest impact rating.
Decking (roof deck)
The plywood or OSB boards the roof is nailed to. It is inspected after tear-off for rot and soft spots.
Deductible
The part of an insurance claim the homeowner pays, set by the policy.
Drip edge
Metal flashing along roof edges that directs water into the gutter and protects the fascia.
Fascia
The board along the roof edge where gutters attach.
Flashing
Metal pieces that seal joints where the roof meets walls, chimneys, skylights and valleys.
Granule loss
Shedding of the mineral granules on asphalt shingles; often visible in gutters after hail or with age.
Hail bruise
A soft spot in a shingle where hail fractured the mat under the granules.
Ice and water shield
Self-adhering waterproof underlayment used at eaves, valleys and penetrations.
Kansas roofing registration
A certificate every roofing contractor providing roofing for a fee in Kansas must hold from the Kansas Attorney General.
MABCD
Metropolitan Area Building and Construction Department, which issues permits in Wichita and Sedgwick County.
Manufacturer warranty
The shingle maker’s coverage for product defects; enhanced tiers can add tear-off and labor coverage.
Modified bitumen
A multi-ply, asphalt-based low-slope roofing system with polymer modifiers.
Pipe boot
A rubber or metal collar that seals around plumbing vent pipes; a frequent leak point.
Pitch
Roof steepness, written as rise per 12 inches of run (for example 6/12).
Ponding water
Water that stays on a low-slope roof more than 48 hours after rain.
Ridge cap
Shingles that cover the peak where two roof planes meet.
Roofing square
100 square feet of roof surface; the unit roofs are measured and priced in.
Soffit
The underside of the roof overhang, often vented to bring air into the attic.
Synthetic underlayment
A woven polymer underlayment installed over the deck, replacing traditional felt paper.
Tear-off
Removing existing roofing down to the deck before installing the new roof.
TPO
Thermoplastic polyolefin, a single-ply white membrane with heat-welded seams used on low-slope roofs.
Valley
The inside angle where two roof planes meet and channel water.
Ventilation (intake and exhaust)
Airflow through soffit intake and ridge or roof exhaust vents that keeps the attic cooler and drier.
Workmanship warranty
The contractor’s coverage for problems caused by installation, separate from the manufacturer’s product warranty.
