Quick Answer

Which Roof Material Fits a Wichita Home?

For most Wichita homes, impact-rated architectural asphalt shingles give the best balance of cost and storm performance.

Metal and synthetic slate cost more but add longevity or a premium look. Commercial low-slope roofs use different systems, usually TPO or modified bitumen. Compare each option below, then let a free inspection confirm what your roof needs.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Still have a question? Call (316) 742-1900.

What is the most popular roof material in Wichita?
Architectural asphalt shingles are the most common choice for Wichita homes.
Is metal roofing good for hail?
Metal sheds water well and lasts a long time, but large hail can dent some panels. Ask about impact ratings and cosmetic-damage terms.
Do commercial roofs use shingles?
Low-slope commercial roofs typically use membrane systems like TPO or modified bitumen instead.
What does a Class 4 shingle rating mean?
A Class 4 shingle passes the UL 2218 impact test with a 2-inch steel ball, the highest impact rating.
How much does a new asphalt roof cost?
Our basic planning price is $505 per roofing square (100 square feet of roof surface). Your final price is confirmed in writing after a free inspection.
