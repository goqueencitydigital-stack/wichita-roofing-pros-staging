Financing Made For Homeowners

Roof repairs and replacements are often unexpected expenses. With Wisetack financing, homeowners can move forward with important roofing work immediately while spreading payments over time with convenient monthly options.

Whether you’re dealing with storm damage, aging shingles, or a full roof replacement, financing can help make the project more manageable without delaying repairs.

FAQ

Got any questions? We’ve got answers.

Still have a question? Call (316) 742-1900.

Will checking my financing options affect my credit score?

No. Checking your eligibility through Wisetack uses a soft credit inquiry, so your credit score will not be affected.

How long does the application take?

Most customers can complete the application and view financing options in about a minute.

Are there any hidden fees or prepayment penalties?

No. Wisetack does not charge hidden fees, compounding interest, or prepayment penalties.

What roofing services can be financed?

Financing may be available for roof repairs, roof replacements, storm damage restoration, gutter services, and other qualifying roofing projects.

How do monthly payments work?

Qualified customers can choose from available payment plans with fixed monthly payments based on approved financing terms.

Can I pay off my financing early?

Yes. You can pay off your balance early without additional penalties or fees.

Who provides the financing?

Financing is provided through Wisetack and its lending partners, subject to credit approval.
