Quick Answer

What Will a New Roof Cost?

Our basic roof replacement price is $505 per roofing square (100 square feet of roof surface). Your final price is confirmed in writing after a free inspection.

Financing is available through Wisetack for qualifying projects, and new roofs include our lifetime transferable workmanship warranty.

Price Factors

What Affects the Price?

Six things move a roofing estimate up or down.

01

Roof size (squares)

The main driver; 1 square = 100 sq ft of roof

02

Pitch and complexity

Steeper roofs and more valleys take longer

03

Layers to remove

Extra layers add tear-off and disposal

04

Decking repairs

Soft or rotted boards are replaced as needed

05

Material

Impact-rated, metal and synthetic cost more

06

Permits

MABCD roofing fee: $0.05 per sq ft, $50 min / $1,500 max

Permit fee source: MABCD Permits.
