What Is LP SmartSide and How Is It Made?

LP SmartSide is a brand of engineered wood siding manufactured by Louisiana-Pacific. It is produced by bonding wood strands with a resin adhesive system under heat and pressure, then treating the surface with a zinc borate compound for added protection, and finally applying a primed overlay surface. The product comes in lap siding, panel, trim, and soffit configurations. It is designed to replicate the appearance of wood without the vulnerability to rot and insect damage that affects real wood siding. The engineered wood construction gives LP SmartSide a visual texture that looks more natural than vinyl profiles and provides a substrate that accepts paint well, allowing homeowners to repaint in any color rather than being limited to the original factory color. LP SmartSide installation in Wichita requires specific installation practices outlined in the LP manufacturer’s installation guide, particularly regarding bottom edge clearances from rooflines and grade, moisture barriers, and fastening requirements.

How Does LP SmartSide Handle Wichita Hail and Wind?

Wichita’s hail and wind exposure is one of the primary performance criteria for any siding material. LP SmartSide lap siding and panels have demonstrated good impact resistance in testing compared to standard vinyl, which can crack, shatter, or deform under hail impact. The layered construction and strand-based core absorb impact energy better than a thin hollow vinyl profile. Wind resistance is dependent on fastening compliance with the LP installation guide, which specifies nail size, penetration depth, and spacing based on local wind design pressures. Wichita falls in a wind design region that requires attention to fastening at panel edges and corners where wind pressure concentrations are highest. According to IBHS hail and high-wind roof research, proper fastening installation according to product specifications is one of the most critical factors determining how well any siding material performs during severe weather. Verification that the installing contractor is following the LP SmartSide installation guide specifically is more important than the general reputation of the material.

Is LP SmartSide Resistant to Moisture in Wichita’s Climate?

LP SmartSide is designed with moisture resistance as a primary engineering goal, achieved through the zinc borate treatment and primed surface. However, the product’s moisture resistance depends entirely on maintaining the paint film and sealed end cuts. Any exposed unprimed wood at field cuts, around windows, at the bottom edge near grade, or at penetrations is a moisture entry point that will cause the product to swell and deteriorate. LP’s warranty requires that all field cuts be sealed with a compatible primer before installation, that bottom edges maintain a minimum clearance of 6 inches from grade and 1 inch from roof surfaces, and that the product be painted within a specified time window after installation. Wichita’s climate includes hot, dry summers that accelerate paint aging and spring rain events that test moisture management. Homes that allow siding to contact roofing materials, sit too close to grade, or go unpainted after installation will experience premature warranty-voiding deterioration regardless of the product’s general moisture resistance rating. A professional siding contractor familiar with LP SmartSide installation in Wichita will follow these clearance requirements during installation rather than treating them as optional.

What Paint and Finish Requirements Does LP SmartSide Have?

LP SmartSide is delivered with a factory-applied primer overlay but requires topcoat painting within 180 days of installation per LP’s warranty terms. The product accepts standard exterior 100% acrylic latex paint, which is the industry standard for most exterior coatings. Color choice is virtually unlimited since the substrate is designed to accept any compatible paint formulation. Two-coat application over the factory primer is the recommended approach for maximum protection and paint adhesion. Repainting on a regular schedule, typically every 7 to 12 years in Wichita depending on color depth, sun exposure, and paint quality, maintains the moisture barrier that the product depends on. Dark colors absorb more heat and can cause paint to age faster on south-facing walls in Wichita’s summer sun. Lighter colors that reflect heat extend the paint film life on high-sun elevations. Because LP SmartSide can be repainted in any color, homeowners have flexibility to change the appearance over time in ways that factory-colored vinyl does not allow. Wichita Roofing Pros can assess the condition of installed LP SmartSide during a roof and exterior inspection and note any areas where paint degradation or sealant failure is beginning.

Top 5 Installation Details That Determine LP SmartSide Performance in Wichita

  1. Bottom edge clearance from rooflines and grade – LP requires a minimum 1-inch clearance between siding and roof surfaces and 6 inches from grade. Violations trap moisture at the base of the panel and are among the most common sources of premature deterioration.
  2. Field cut sealing – Every cut edge must be primed with a compatible LP End Cut Sealer before installation. Unsealed cuts at window openings, trim cuts, and notches are immediate moisture vulnerabilities.
  3. Fastening pattern compliance – LP specifies nail size, type, and spacing. Insufficient fastening creates panels that flex under wind pressure and can allow water infiltration at the back side of the panel.
  4. Moisture barrier integration – House wrap or building paper must be installed correctly behind LP SmartSide as a secondary drainage plane. Direct installation over bare sheathing without a moisture barrier is a warranty violation that also compromises moisture performance.
  5. Paint application timing – The 180-day paint window after installation is not just a recommendation; it is a warranty condition. Delays in painting expose the primer surface to UV degradation that affects paint adhesion for the service life of the coating.

How Does LP SmartSide Compare to Vinyl Siding on Wichita Homes?

Vinyl siding requires no painting, does not rot, and has lower upfront cost than LP SmartSide. LP SmartSide costs more to purchase and install, requires periodic repainting, and the warranty is more maintenance-dependent. However, LP SmartSide delivers a wood-grain visual appearance that vinyl cannot replicate convincingly at mid-range price points, and it performs better under hail and impact loads than standard vinyl profiles. For Wichita homeowners who prioritize aesthetics, particularly on homes in established neighborhoods where a wood-look exterior is more consistent with surrounding architecture, LP SmartSide offers an appearance upgrade with manageable maintenance demands. For homeowners who want absolute minimum exterior maintenance and are comfortable with the look of vinyl, standard vinyl siding with a Class 4 impact rating is the lower-maintenance alternative. Both products serve the Wichita market well when installed correctly by a qualified contractor. Reviewing your Wichita service area options for siding contractors who carry certified installation experience with LP SmartSide is an important vetting step.

What Warranty Does LP SmartSide Carry and What Does It Cover?

LP SmartSide products are backed by a SmartGuard 5/50 warranty: a 5-year 100% coverage period followed by prorated coverage out to 50 years against manufacturing defects, fungal decay, and termite damage. The warranty is transferable to subsequent owners, which can provide a modest resale benefit. The warranty does not cover damage from improper installation, failure to paint within the required window, bottom-edge clearance violations, or mechanical damage from hail or storm events. Storm damage is a homeowner’s insurance matter rather than a manufacturer warranty claim, which is an important distinction when evaluating the full risk picture in Wichita’s active storm climate. Sedgwick County roofing and exterior permits, administered through the Sedgwick County MABCD permits office, apply to siding work that involves significant structural or envelope modifications, and verifying permit requirements before a project starts protects the homeowner’s warranty standing as well.

Why Wichita siding takes a beating

Hail, wind-blown debris and big temperature swings are hard on siding. Cracks and loose panels let wind-driven rain behind the wall, and damage near rooflines can show up as roof-edge leaks.

Siding warning signs

Look closely at the sides that face prevailing wind and storms.

  • Cracked, split or hail-dented panels
  • Warped or buckled sections
  • Soft or swollen boards near the ground or roof line
  • Peeling paint on trim
  • Gaps where siding meets windows or roof lines
  • Mold or mildew streaks
  • Loose or missing pieces after wind
  • Interior wall stains on exterior walls

Frequently Asked Questions About LP SmartSide in Wichita

Does LP SmartSide hold up to Wichita hail better than vinyl?

LP SmartSide’s engineered wood construction provides better impact resistance than standard vinyl profiles under moderate hail conditions. For extreme large-hail events, any siding material may sustain damage, but LP SmartSide typically shows denting or surface damage rather than the cracking and shattering that thin vinyl can experience. Hail damage to LP SmartSide may be covered under homeowner’s insurance as storm damage rather than a warranty claim, so documenting damage promptly after events is important.

How long does LP SmartSide last before needing to be repainted in Wichita?

In Wichita’s climate, LP SmartSide typically needs repainting every 7 to 12 years depending on paint quality, color, and sun exposure. South-facing walls with deep color absorb more UV and heat, which accelerates paint aging. Using a high-quality 100% acrylic exterior paint in lighter reflective tones on high-sun elevations extends the repaint interval. Neglecting repainting beyond 12 to 15 years risks moisture infiltration that voids the manufacturer warranty.

Can LP SmartSide be installed in winter in Wichita?

LP SmartSide can be installed in cold weather with some adjustments. Sealants and caulks require minimum temperature thresholds for proper adhesion, typically above 40 degrees Fahrenheit. Paint application should be deferred to spring unless temperatures remain consistently above manufacturer minimums. Fastening and panel installation are not significantly affected by cold weather, but the complete finish process is best scheduled for milder conditions.

Is LP SmartSide available in different profiles and textures?

Yes. LP SmartSide is available in lap siding in multiple widths, vertical panel configurations, trim and fascia profiles, and soffit panels. Texture options include smooth, cedar mill finish, and rougher woodgrain profiles that provide different visual effects. The variety of profiles allows LP SmartSide to be used consistently across the full exterior envelope including rake trim, corner boards, and window surrounds, creating a cohesive appearance that is difficult to achieve with mixed materials.

What should I ask a Wichita siding contractor before hiring for LP SmartSide?

Ask specifically whether the contractor is familiar with LP SmartSide installation requirements including end-cut sealing, clearance requirements from grade and rooflines, and moisture barrier integration. Request references from LP SmartSide projects completed in the prior two years. Confirm the contractor pulls required permits and that installation will be done to the LP installation guide specifications. A warranty on labor separate from the product warranty is also reasonable to request, as installation errors are not covered by LP’s product warranty.

LP SmartSide is a capable siding product for Wichita homes when it is specified correctly and installed by a contractor who follows the manufacturer’s requirements. Its performance depends more on installation quality and maintenance discipline than on the material itself. For homeowners who want the wood aesthetic, the improved impact resistance over vinyl, and the flexibility to repaint in any color over time, it is a well-suited option for the Wichita exterior environment.
