Cowley County Storms

What storms are part of Cowley County's history?

Cowley County sits on the Oklahoma line, where storms crossing the southern plains often reach Kansas first.

01

May 25, 1955: the Udall tornado

Shortly after 10:30 p.m. on May 25, 1955, a tornado struck the small town of Udall in northwest Cowley County without warning. The Kansas Historical Society reports that 192 buildings and 170 homes were lost and the death toll eventually reached 87.

The National Weather Service report describes destruction across the full width of the town, about three-fourths of a mile. It remains the deadliest tornado in Kansas history.

02

May 31, 2008: hail up to 4.25 inches

During the predawn hail event of May 31, 2008, parts of Cowley County saw hail of 2.75 to 4.25 inches, according to the NWS in Wichita. Burden, Dexter and New Salem were among the communities hit especially hard.

Hail that large punctures shingles, splits wood and breaks skylights. Many east Cowley County roofs were replaced after that storm and are now well into their second decade.

03

Storms from Oklahoma

Supercells that form over north-central Oklahoma often track northeast into Cowley County. Arkansas City and Winfield sit right in that path.

That is one reason Cowley County homeowners should check the roof after any storm with hail, even small hail, and keep photos of the roof before storm season.

04

River valleys and wind

The Arkansas and Walnut rivers meet near Arkansas City, and the river valleys channel wind through both towns. Older trees along the rivers drop limbs in high wind.

We check for impact damage from branches as well as hail and wind, and we recommend trimming limbs that hang over the roof.

Cowley County Permits

How do roofing permits work in Cowley County?

Winfield and Arkansas City run their own building offices, and the county handles the unincorporated areas.

01

Winfield requires a re-roofing permit and inspection

The City of Winfield Building and Construction page says a permit and inspection are required for re-roofing to verify code compliance. Contractors must be licensed, insured and bonded with the city, and roofing contractors must provide a copy of their state roofing registration.

Under Winfield city code, the residential re-roofing permit fee is $15 when the roofing material installed is 100 square feet or more.

02

Winfield inspections

Winfield inspection requests go to (620) 221-5600 at least 24 hours before the requested date. The city asks for the permit number, address, contact name and inspection type, and a company representative must be present.

The permit holder must also request a final inspection when work is complete. Public Improvements is on the second floor of City Hall at 200 E. 9th Ave.

03

Arkansas City

Arkansas City requires permits for construction activity unless a building code exception applies. Its permit procedures page says roofing must be performed by licensed contractors, even when a landlord pulls other permits.

Inspections must be scheduled at least 24 hours ahead, and a final inspection is required at the end. Neighborhood Services is at 118 W. Central Ave., (620) 441-4400.

04

Unincorporated Cowley County

The Cowley County FAQ says a building permit is needed for construction involving repair of any building, with fees based on the cost of construction.

The Cowley County Courthouse is at 311 E. 9th Avenue in Winfield, (620) 221-5400. Udall, Burden, Dexter and other small towns handle permits through their city offices.

Cowley County Homes

What do Cowley County roofs need most?

Cowley County housing is older on average than the Wichita suburbs, and that changes the work.

01

Plank decking under old roofs

Many Winfield and Ark City homes built before 1960 have board decking with gaps meant for wood shingles. We add new sheathing where needed so modern shingles can be nailed properly.

02

Multiple layers

Older Cowley County homes often carry two layers of shingles. We remove every layer, which lets us inspect the deck and reduces weight on the framing.

03

Chimneys and flashing

Brick chimneys on older homes need new step and counter flashing during a replacement. We do not rely on caulk to seal a chimney.

04

Impact-resistant upgrades

After storms like 2008, many Cowley County homeowners ask about Class 4 impact-resistant shingles. Ask your insurance agent whether they offer a discount.

How does a Cowley County roof project come together?

Winfield and Arkansas City are a longer drive from our Wichita office, so we plan Cowley County projects carefully.

Inspection day. We group Cowley County inspections so we can spend real time on each roof. Expect photos of every slope, the attic where we can reach it, gutters and any outbuildings you ask us to look at.

The right city or county office. Winfield requires a permit and inspection for every reroof, Arkansas City requires licensed roofing contractors and scheduled inspections, and the county handles unincorporated property. We confirm which applies before writing the estimate.

Written estimate. Our planning price is $505 per roofing square. A 25-square Cowley County home starts near $12,625, and the final price is confirmed in writing after the inspection. Older Winfield and Ark City homes often need new sheathing over plank decking, and that is listed separately.

Licensing, bonding and insurance. Winfield requires contractors to be licensed, insured and bonded with the city before work starts. We have that paperwork in place before we schedule your tear-off.

Install and inspections. We call Winfield inspections at least 24 hours ahead and have a company representative on site, and we follow the same 24-hour lead time in Arkansas City. A final inspection closes out the permit.

Walkthrough and warranty. Cleanup, magnet sweep and a final walkthrough, backed by our lifetime transferable workmanship warranty.

What roofing problems are common in Cowley County?

Common roofing problems around Cowley County include storm-lifted shingles, hail marks, worn pipe boots, flashing leaks, clogged gutters, and water moving behind fascia or siding. In our experience, small stains inside the home often start outside at a detail that looks minor from the ground. That is why we check the roof, drainage, and nearby exterior components together.

What roofing service should I choose first in Cowley County?
Start with roof repair if you have an active leak, a few missing shingles, or isolated flashing damage. Look at roof replacement when the roof is aging, leaking repeatedly, or showing damage across several slopes. An inspection should make that choice clearer.
