What Happens During a Free Roof Inspection?

A free roof inspection checks the roof surface, flashing, vents, valleys, gutters and visible storm damage, and most take around 30–60 minutes. You get photos and a clear explanation before any work is proposed.

  1. Book Choose a time online or call (316) 742-1900. Hablamos Español.
  2. We listen first Tell us what you have noticed — a stain, a leak, missing shingles or a recent storm.
  3. Exterior and roof check Shingles, flashing, pipe boots, vents, valleys, ridges, gutters and soft metals.
  4. Photos When access is safe, we photograph what we find.
  5. Plain-English findings What needs attention now, what can be monitored, and why.
  6. Written next step If work is recommended, scope and price are provided in writing. No obligation.

FAQ

Got any questions? We’ve got answers.

Still have a question? Call (316) 742-1900.

Is the roof inspection completely free?
Yes. Our roof inspections are 100% free with no hidden fees or obligation to purchase any services.
What does the free roof inspection include?
Our inspection includes a full evaluation of your roof’s condition, including shingles, flashing, gutters, leaks, storm damage, and other visible roofing issues.
How long does the inspection take?
Most inspections take around 30–60 minutes depending on the size and condition of your roof.
Do I need to be home during the inspection?
Not always. In many cases we can complete the exterior inspection without you being present, then discuss the results afterward.
Will you provide photos of roof damage?
Yes. If we find any issues, we can provide photos and explain the condition of your roof in detail.
