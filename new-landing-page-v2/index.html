DON’T IGNORE THESE WARNING SIGNS

Your Roof May Be Asking for Help

Contact Wichita Roofing Pros if you’ve noticed:

  • Missing, cracked, curled, or loose shingles
  • Dark patches or visible roof discoloration
  • Water stains on ceilings or walls
  • Granules collecting in gutters
  • Damaged flashing around vents or chimneys
  • Sagging or uneven sections of the roof
  • Rising heating or cooling costs
  • Damage following hail or strong winds
  • A roof approaching the end of its expected lifespan
  • Recurring repairs that never seem to solve the problem

Even if you haven’t noticed any of these signs, recent severe weather may still have caused damage that isn’t visible from the ground.

FAQs

Your Roofing Concerns Answered

Still have a question? Call (316) 247-0803.

How long does a roof replacement take?

Most residential roof replacements are completed within 1–2 days, depending on the size and complexity of your home and the roofing materials selected. Our team works efficiently to minimize disruption and ensures everything is properly cleaned up before we leave.

Do you handle insurance claims for storm damage

Yes. We can help guide you through the insurance claim process after storm damage. Our team can inspect your roof, document the damage, and provide the information needed to help you work with your insurance company.

Can you assist with a roofing insurance claim?

Absolutely. We can assist with the roofing portion of your insurance claim by documenting the damage, providing a detailed assessment, and helping you understand the repair or replacement process. While we don’t determine your coverage, we’ll help make the process as straightforward as possible.

How long does roof replacement take?

Most roof replacements can be completed in 1–2 days, though the timeline can vary based on the roof’s size, complexity, weather conditions, and materials. We’ll provide you with a clear timeline before work begins.

Do I have to replace my roof after the inspection?

Not necessarily. An inspection doesn’t automatically mean your roof needs to be replaced. We’ll assess its condition and clearly explain whether repairs, maintenance, or a full replacement is the best option. Our goal is to recommend what’s actually needed—not sell you something you don’t need.

Do you use subcontractors?

We may work with trusted, experienced subcontractors when needed to ensure projects are completed efficiently and to our quality standards. Any work performed on your roof is carefully managed and held to the same standards we expect from our own team.
