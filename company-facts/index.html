Services

What does Wichita Roofing Pros do?

Wichita Roofing Pros is a residential and commercial roofing contractor that also installs gutters and siding. Core services include:

  • Roof repair: leak detection and repair, storm and hail damage repair, emergency roof repair, emergency tarping and roof maintenance
  • Roof replacement: full tear-off replacements, insurance claim replacements and financed replacements
  • Residential roofing: architectural asphalt shingles, metal roofing, synthetic slate and shake, concrete tile and attic ventilation
  • Commercial roofing: TPO, modified bitumen, flat roof repair, and commercial inspections and maintenance
  • Gutters: seamless gutter installation, gutter repair and gutter guards
  • Siding and trim: vinyl siding, LP SmartSide, and soffit, fascia and trim

Free roof inspections are available for every service. See all roofing services.

Pricing

How does Wichita Roofing Pros price a roof?

For planning, the company uses a price of $505 per roofing square, where one square equals 100 square feet of roof surface. This is a planning price only. The final price is confirmed in writing after a free inspection measures the roof and checks layers, decking, flashing, ventilation and material choice.

Homeowners can estimate their own roof with the roof cost calculator, which multiplies home footprint by a pitch factor to estimate squares. Permits in Wichita and areas served by MABCD are typically $0.05 per square foot of roof, with a $50 minimum and $1,500 maximum, and are listed in the written estimate.

Financing

Does Wichita Roofing Pros offer financing?

Yes, through Wisetack. Qualifying homeowners can finance $500 to $25,000* over 3 to 60 months*, with APRs from 0% to 35.9%*. Checking eligibility uses a soft credit check that does not affect your credit score. All financing is subject to credit approval. See the financing page for details.

Warranty

What warranty comes with a new roof?

Every new roof includes a lifetime transferable workmanship warranty from Wichita Roofing Pros. The warranty can transfer once to a new owner, as long as the transfer is requested within 30 days of the sale and a warranty inspection is completed. TAMKO system warranty upgrades are also available on qualifying roofs. Details are on the lifetime transferable warranty page.

Inspections

What happens during a free roof inspection?

A free inspection usually takes about 30 to 60 minutes. The inspector checks the roof surface, flashing, vents, gutters and, where accessible, the attic. You receive photos and a plain-English explanation of what was found. There is no obligation. If work is needed, you receive a written scope and price. Book a free inspection.

Storm Work

Does Wichita Roofing Pros help with insurance claims?

Yes. The company inspects storm and hail damage, documents it with photos and helps homeowners understand what was found before they talk with their insurer. The insurance company and its adjuster decide coverage. Wichita Roofing Pros can meet the adjuster at the property when helpful and complete the approved repair or replacement. Learn more about insurance claim roof replacement and the storm and hail resource center.

Emergency Help

Does Wichita Roofing Pros offer emergency roof repair?

Yes. The company provides emergency roof repair and emergency roof tarping to stop active leaks and protect the home until permanent repairs can be made. Call (316) 742-1900 during office hours.
