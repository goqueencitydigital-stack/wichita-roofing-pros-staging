Quick Answer

Just Had Hail or Wind in Wichita?

After a Wichita hail or wind storm, stay safe, stop any active leak, photograph damage from the ground and schedule a free roof inspection. If damage is found, report it to your insurer.

The insurance company decides coverage; your contractor documents damage and performs the approved work. This page walks you through each step, what to look for, and how to avoid the storm chasers who show up after every big storm.

What To Do

Step by Step After a Storm

Seven steps, in the order that protects your home and your claim.

Right after the storm

  1. 01
    Stay safe.

    Stay off the roof and away from downed lines, broken glass and exposed nails.

  2. 02
    Stop active water.

    Call for emergency tarping if water is coming in. Photograph the damage before any temporary fix.

In the first few days

  1. 03
    Document.

    Date-stamped photos of the roof from the ground, gutters, siding, windows and any interior stains.

  2. 04
    Inspect.

    Book a free roof inspection with photos so you know what is actually on the roof.

  3. 05
    Report.

    If damage is found, contact your insurance agent to start the claim.

When the claim moves

  1. 06
    Meet the adjuster.

    We can attend the adjuster inspection and walk the roof together.

  2. 07
    Choose and schedule.

    You choose the contractor. Approved work is scheduled and confirmed in writing.

Check From the Ground

Signs of Storm Damage You Can Spot Safely

You do not need to climb a ladder to spot the first clues. Walk the property in daylight and note anything on this list.

  • Dents or dings on gutters, downspouts, vents and window wraps
  • Granules collecting at downspout outlets or on driveways
  • Missing, creased or lifted shingles, especially on the windward side
  • Dented AC condenser fins or damaged patio furniture
  • Cracked or split siding and damaged window screens
  • New water stains on ceilings or walls after the storm
  • Debris or tree limbs resting on the roof
  • Shingle pieces in the yard

Spotted one or more? Book a free inspection and we will check the roof up close.

Contractor vs. Insurer

Who Does What in a Storm Claim?

A contractor cannot decide your coverage. Here is how responsibilities usually divide.

You, the homeowner

  • Stay safe and stop active leaks
  • Photograph damage from the ground
  • Report the claim to your insurer
  • Optional: inspect the roof yourself — safer to let a pro do it
  • Choose your contractor

Wichita Roofing Pros

  • Emergency tarping
  • Roof-level inspection and photo documentation
  • Help you understand what we found
  • Meet the adjuster on the roof
  • Perform the approved work

Your insurance company

  • Opens the claim
  • Sends an adjuster to inspect
  • Reviews the evidence
  • Decides coverage under your policy
  • Pays per your policy
Document Everything

Build a Claim File That Holds Up

The Kansas Insurance Department recommends documenting all damage, photographing it before permanent fixes, and keeping your paperwork somewhere easy to find. Here is a simple checklist.

Photograph

  • Each side of the house and roof from the ground
  • Dented gutters, downspouts, vents and AC fins
  • Damaged siding, screens and window wraps
  • Interior ceiling or wall stains
  • Hail on the ground next to a coin or ruler, if it is still there

Keep

  • The date and time of the storm
  • Receipts for tarps and temporary repairs — they may be reimbursable
  • Your claim number and adjuster’s contact details
  • Emails and notes from your agent, adjuster and contractor
  • Every written estimate and scope
Avoid Storm Chasers

How to Vet a Roofer After a Storm

Out-of-town crews follow every big Kansas hail storm. These checks take minutes and protect your home and your claim.

01

Check Kansas registration

Every roofing contractor working in Kansas must be registered with the Attorney General. Look them up in the roofing registration directory.

02

Look for a local address

Ask where their office is and how long they have been in Wichita. We are at 1809 S Hoover Rd Suite 101.

03

Get more than one bid

The Kansas Insurance Department suggests getting more than one bid and checking trusted sources.

04

Don’t sign under pressure

Be skeptical of anyone pushing you to sign over your assignment of benefits — it lets them deal with your insurer without you.

05

Get the scope in writing

Never sign before you understand exactly what will be done, with what materials, and for what price.

06

Know the warning signs

Door-knockers “in the area,” leftover-materials offers, cash-only or pay-everything-up-front deals, and pressure to decide now are classic red flags. See the FTC’s guide.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Still have a question? Call (316) 742-1900.

Does my roofer decide what insurance covers?
No. Your insurance company decides coverage under your policy. Wichita Roofing Pros documents damage and can meet the adjuster.
How can I check a roofer is registered in Kansas?
Use the Kansas Attorney General’s roofing registration directory. Every roofing contractor working in Kansas must be registered.
Should I sign with a storm chaser?
Be cautious. Check registration, local address and references, and never sign before you understand the scope.
Do you offer emergency tarping?
Yes. Call (316) 742-1900 if water is coming in.
What size hail is considered severe?
The National Weather Service treats hail one inch across (about the size of a quarter) or larger as severe. Hail size is only one clue; roof age, material and wind all matter, so an inspection confirms the actual damage.
Should I make temporary repairs before the adjuster comes?
If water is getting in, protect the home. The Kansas Insurance Department advises taking photos before doing any permanent fixes and keeping receipts for temporary repairs, which may be reimbursable.
Is the storm inspection really free?
Yes. The roof inspection is free and there is no obligation. You get photos and a plain-English explanation of what we found.
