Wichita Storm & Hail Resource Center

What to do after hail or wind, how to document damage, and how responsibilities divide between you, your contractor and your insurer.

Schedule a Free Roof InspectionCall (316) 742-1900

Quick answerAfter a Wichita hail or wind storm, stay safe, stop any active leak, photograph damage from the ground and schedule a free roof inspection. If damage is found, report it to your insurer. The insurance company decides coverage; your contractor documents damage and performs the approved work.

Contractor vs. insurer

Who does what in a storm claim?

A contractor cannot decide your coverage. Here is how responsibilities usually divide.

What to do

Step by step after a storm

  1. Stay safe. Stay off the roof and away from downed lines.
  2. Stop active water. Call for emergency tarping if needed.
  3. Document. Date-stamped photos of roof, gutters, siding and interior.
  4. Inspect. Book a free roof inspection with photos.
  5. Report. Contact your insurer if damage is found.
  6. Meet the adjuster. We can attend and walk the roof.
  7. Choose and schedule. Approved work is scheduled in writing.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Still have a question? Call (316) 742-1900.

Does my roofer decide what insurance covers?
No. Your insurance company decides coverage under your policy. Wichita Roofing Pros documents damage and can meet the adjuster.
How can I check a roofer is registered in Kansas?
Use the Kansas Attorney General’s roofing registration directory. Every roofing contractor working in Kansas must be registered.
Should I sign with a storm chaser?
Be cautious. Check registration, local address and references, and never sign before you understand the scope.
Do you offer emergency tarping?
Yes. Call (316) 742-1900 if water is coming in.
