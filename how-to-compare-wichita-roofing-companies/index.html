Search for roofing companies near me in Wichita and you will get a long list: established local contractors, newer companies and, after a big storm, crews from out of state. Comparing Wichita roofing companies is less about finding the lowest number and more about making sure every roofer is bidding on the same roof, with the same protections behind the work. This guide walks through a simple side-by-side process any Wichita homeowner can use, whether you need a small repair or a full roof replacement.

Start With Registration and Insurance in Kansas

Kansas does not issue a separate state roofing license. Instead, roofing contractors must register with the Kansas Attorney General, and registration requires proof of liability insurance of not less than $500,000. That makes the first check quick: look up each company in the Kansas Attorney General roofing registration directory. If a roofer is not listed, stop there.

Ask each company for a current certificate of insurance and confirm the business name on it matches the name on your estimate. If you are in Wichita or elsewhere in Sedgwick County, your project may also need a permit; our guide to roofing permits in Wichita and Sedgwick County explains who issues them.

Check for a Local Office and Real Wichita References

A local address matters most after the job is done. Leaks, flashing questions and warranty calls can come up years after the crew leaves, and you want a company that is still easy to reach. Ask every roofer for their office address, their regular business hours and a few nearby jobs you can drive past.

  • A physical office in the Wichita area you can visit
  • A phone number answered during business hours
  • Recent reviews that mention specific crews or projects
  • Photos of finished roofs on homes like yours
  • Clear answers about who does the work
  • Written warranty terms you can read before signing

Compare Wichita Roofers on the Same Written Scope

Two estimates can look far apart simply because they describe different jobs. One bid may include a full tear-off, new underlayment, new flashing and ventilation, while another quietly assumes a layover or leaves out decking. Before you compare prices, line the estimates up item by item.

What to compare on each roofing estimate
Line itemWhat to ask each roofer
Roof sizeHow many roofing squares did you measure?
Tear-offAre all existing layers removed down to the deck?
DeckingHow is damaged decking found and priced?
Flashing and ventsIs flashing replaced or reused? Is ventilation checked?
MaterialsWhich shingle line and underlayment are included?
CleanupAre magnet sweeps and debris haul-off included?
WarrantyWhat workmanship and manufacturer coverage comes with it?

A roofing square is 100 square feet of roof surface, and most pricing is built from that measurement. If two roofers measured very different square counts, ask why. Our guides on what a roofing square is and how to read a written roofing estimate go deeper.

Ask About Crews, Credentials and Who Does the Work

Ask every Wichita roofing company who will actually be on your roof and who you call if something goes wrong. Some companies use their own crews; others sub out the work. Neither answer is automatically wrong, but you should know who is responsible for quality and cleanup.

Manufacturer credentials can also tell you something. Shingle makers run contractor programs with their own requirements, and some warranty upgrades are only available through certified installers. Compare Warranties, Not Just Prices

Warranties are where estimates that look similar often differ most. A manufacturer warranty covers defects in the materials. A workmanship warranty covers the installation itself, which is where most early roof problems start. Ask each roofer what is covered, for how long, whether it transfers if you sell, and what maintenance it requires.

Every new roof from Wichita Roofing Pros includes our lifetime transferable workmanship warranty, with optional TAMKO system upgrades. Whatever company you choose, get the warranty terms in writing before you sign.

Red Flags When Comparing Roofing Companies Near You

Most problems with roofing contractors show up in the sales process before any work starts. The FTC guidance on home improvement scams warns about door-to-door offers, high-pressure tactics and large upfront payments.

  • No listing in the Kansas AG roofing registration directory
  • Pressure to sign before an inspection or adjuster visit
  • Offers to waive or cover your insurance deductible
  • Full payment requested before work begins
  • A verbal price with no written scope
  • No local office or a phone number that changes

After hail, out-of-area crews often knock on doors across Wichita, Derby, Andover and the rest of Sedgwick County. Our storm and hail resource center covers what to do before you sign anything after a storm.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many roofing estimates should I get in Wichita?

There is no set number, but two or three written estimates are usually enough to see whether the scope and price are reasonable. What matters most is that each estimate covers the same work.

Is a roofing license required in Kansas?

Kansas requires roofing contractors to register with the Attorney General rather than hold a separate state license. Registration requires proof of liability insurance, and you can look up any contractor in the AG roofing registration directory.

Should I choose the cheapest roofing company?

Only if its scope, materials and warranty match the other bids. A lower price that leaves out tear-off, decking, flashing or cleanup can cost more later.

What should a roofing estimate include?

Measured roof size, tear-off, decking allowance, flashing, ventilation, the shingle line and underlayment, cleanup, warranty terms, permit handling and payment terms.
