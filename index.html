Who Stands Behind Your Installation?

Jay from Wichita Roofing Pros explains our lifetime transferable workmanship warranty. The manufacturer stands behind its product. Wichita Roofing Pros stands behind our installation.

  • Manufacturer warranty Covers the roofing products, provided by the manufacturer.

FAQ

Got any questions? We’ve got answers.

Still have a question? Call (316) 742-1900.

How do I know if my roof needs repair or full replacement?

Signs like recurring leaks, missing shingles, storm damage, sagging areas, or an older roof may indicate it’s time for a professional inspection. A roofing expert can determine whether targeted repairs or a full replacement is the better long-term solution.

Do you provide free roof inspections in Wichita, KS?

Yes. A roof inspection helps identify leaks, storm-related damage, worn materials, and other issues before they become larger and more expensive problems.

Can you help with insurance claims after hail or storm damage?

Yes. If your roof has been affected by hail, wind, or severe weather, the team can inspect the damage, document findings, and help guide you through the insurance claim process.

How long does a roof replacement usually take?

Most residential roof replacements can often be completed within one to a few days, depending on the size of the roof, material type, weather conditions, and the scope of the project.

Do you offer residential and commercial roofing services?

Yes. Wichita Roofing Pros works with both homeowners and commercial property owners, providing repairs, replacements, inspections, installations, and ongoing roofing solutions.
